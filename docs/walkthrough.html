Field Walkthrough

LocaTrack

Your parts stockroom, in your pocket. It tells you what you've got, where it is - the shop, the truck, or a job site - and what each job has used. No spreadsheets, no guessing, no "I thought we had another box."

This guide walks through it the way you'd use it on a normal day. No computer know-how needed. If you can use a phone, you can use this.

Start here

The big idea

Think of a paper parts ledger - but it adds itself up. Only three words to learn:

PART

What it is

One kind of item you stock - a 20A breaker, a roll of 14/2, a box of wire nuts. A Part is just the name and details, not a count.

LOCATION

Where it is

The Shop, a Truck, or a Job Site. Parts are always counted at a place - never "somewhere."

STOCK

How many

The count of one Part at one Location. You never type this number. The app works it out from what you record.

Part+Location=Stock

The one habit that makes it work

You don't change a number. You record what happened: "Used 3 on the Main St job." "Received 10 from the supplier." "Moved 5 from shop to truck." The app does the math. That's why two people can use it at once without overwriting each other - every action is added to the history and nothing is ever erased.

Where your stuff lives

Three kinds of place

🏭
Shop

Main stockroom. Deliveries land here.

🚚

What you carry out. One per vehicle.

🏠

Materials left on location for a job.

Supplier ▶ Shop ▶ Truck ▶ Job Site ▶ Used

Every step along that line is something you can record in the app. Old places are archived, not deleted, so you can still look back at what a finished job used.

Finding your way around

Four tabs along the bottom

Like the buttons on a tool belt - same spot every time. Tap one to switch screens.

📦 Parts

Your whole catalogue. Search it, tap a part to see the count at each place, and do most of your day's work from here.

📷 Scan

Point the camera at a barcode or a LocaTrack QR label and the app jumps straight to that part.

⚠️ Low stock

Your "go buy more" list. Every part that's down to its reorder number.

⚙️ Setup Owner only

Places, jobs, lists, sharing with your partner, importing/exporting, the cost report. Technicians don't see this tab.

Flow 1

Find a part

Two ways. Typing is fine; scanning is faster.

Option A - Search

  1. Tap Parts at the bottom.
  2. Pull the list down slightly and type a few letters of the name - "breaker", "14/2".
  3. Tap the part. You'll see how many are at each place and the total.

Option B - Scan

  1. Tap Scan. Allow the camera the first time it asks.
  2. Hold the phone over the barcode on the box - or a LocaTrack QR label you stuck on a bin.
  3. The part opens by itself.
No barcode on it (loose wire, bulk screws)? Open the part and tap Show / print QR label. Print it, stick it on the bin, and from then on it scans like anything else.
Flow 2 · the one you'll do most

Use a part on a job

You grabbed three breakers off the truck for the Main St panel swap. Here's how to take them out of the count - and charge them to that job.

  1. Find the part (search or scan) and open it.
  2. Tap Move stock.
  3. Set Type to Use.
  4. Pick Location - where you took it from (e.g. Truck 1).
  5. Enter the quantity: 3.
  6. Under Job, pick Main St Panel Swap from the list. Job not there yet? Tap ＋ New job and type its name right there.
  7. Tap Save. Done - the truck count drops by 3.
Made a mistake? Right after saving you'll see an Undo button. Tap it quickly. Later on, don't edit - just record a fix (see "Fix a count").
The ＋ / − quick buttons on a part's row (Received +1 / Used −1) are shortcuts for one-at-a-time changes at your current location.

Flow 3 · fastest way to load a job

Pick several parts to one job

Loading up for a whole job? Don't do it one part at a time. Fill a basket, then post it all at once.

  1. On the Parts tab, tap the briefcase 💼 button at the top.
  2. Choose the job (or make a new one) and the location you're pulling from.
  3. Tap parts to add them and set each quantity. They collect in a basket - nothing is recorded yet.
  4. Look it over. Tap Keep picking to add more, or Commit when it's right.
  5. Commit records every item as Used on that job in one go.
Not ready? Discard throws the basket away and nothing changes.
Flow 4

Receive a delivery

The supplier dropped off a box of 20A breakers at the shop.

  1. Scan the box or search the part. Open it.
  2. Tap Move stock → Type: Receive.
  3. Location: Shop. Quantity: how many came in.
  4. Save. The shop count goes up.
A brand-new item you've never stocked? Only an Owner can add a new Part (the ＋ at the top of Parts). Technicians ask an Owner - this keeps the catalogue clean, with no three spellings of the same breaker.
Flow 5

Move parts between places

Restocking the truck from the shop? That's a Transfer.

  1. Open the part → Move stock → Type: Transfer.
  2. From: Shop. To: Truck 1. Enter the quantity.
  3. Save. Shop goes down, truck goes up - together, in one step, so the totals always add up.
Leftovers coming back from a job site? Same thing: transfer From the job site To the truck.
Flow 6

Fix a count that's wrong

Walked the shelf and there are 11, but the app says 14? Don't hunt for a number to edit - there isn't one. Record an Adjustment.

  1. Open the part → Move stock → Type: Adjust.
  2. Pick the Location you counted.
  3. Enter the change (−3 to go from 14 to 11).
  4. Write a short note - "Cycle count" or "Damaged, tossed." The note is required.
  5. Save.
Nothing is ever deleted from the history. A correction is a new line that says what changed and why - that's what keeps the records honest.
Flow 7

Know what to reorder

Each part has a reorder number set by an Owner. When the total across all places falls to that number or below, it lands on the Low stock tab, and (if turned on) the app can tell you.

  1. Tap Low stock.
  2. Read the list top to bottom - that's your supply-house order.
  3. When the delivery arrives, Receive it (Flow 4) and the part drops off the list automatically.
Alerts: Setup → Notifications → Low stock alerts. You're told once when a part hits its reorder point, and again only if it recovers and drops again - no nagging.

Owners only

What did that job cost?

Every time a part is Used and tagged to a job, the cost is tallied against that job. When it's time to bill:

  1. Setup → Job costs.
  2. Choose the Job and the Period.
  3. See every part used, the quantity, the unit cost and the job total.
  4. Export it as a spreadsheet (CSV) if you want it for an invoice.
This is why tagging the job on every "Use" matters. Untagged use still lowers the count, but won't show up on any job's bill.

Working together

Two phones, one inventory

LocaTrack keeps its own copy on your phone and shares it through iCloud, so you and your partner see the same counts.

Works with no signal

Basements, crawlspaces, rural sites - keep recording. It syncs when you're back online.

Both can edit at once

If you both use the same part offline, both changes count. Neither one overwrites the other.

Sharing is a one-time invite

Owner: Setup → iCloud & Sharing → Share inventory, send the invite. Your partner accepts it on their own iPhone.

Sharing needs an iCloud account signed in on the phone. If Share inventory is greyed out, check Settings → [your name] → iCloud first.
First-time setup · Owner

Getting started

  1. Places. Setup → Locations: add your Shop, each Truck, any standing job site.
  2. Parts. Add them with ＋ on Parts, or bring in a whole list: Setup → Import spreadsheet (CSV). Export blank template gives you the right columns to fill in on a computer.
  3. Reorder numbers. On each part, set when it counts as "low."
  4. Jobs. Setup → Jobs - or just add them on the fly when you "Use" a part.
  5. Starting counts. Record a Receive for what's on the shelves today (or put quantities in the spreadsheet).
  6. Invite your partner. See "Two phones" above.
Danger zone (bottom of Setup): Empty inventory and Reset to starter inventory wipe everything, on your phone and in iCloud, and can't be undone. Leave them alone unless you really mean it.
When something looks off

If you get stuck

The count is wrong

Record an Adjustment with a note (Flow 6). You can't edit the number directly - by design.

I used parts but forgot to pick a job

The count is still right, but the job's cost report will be short. Tell an Owner so they know the cost isn't captured for that one.

I can't find a part I know we carry

Try fewer letters, or scan the box. If it truly isn't there, an Owner needs to add it.

I don't see prices or a Setup tab

Normal for a Technician. Cost and setup are Owner-only.

My partner's numbers are different from mine

Give both phones a minute with signal and iCloud on - they catch up on their own. Still different? Check Setup → iCloud sync (Owners) and make sure both are signed in to iCloud.

I deleted something by accident

Jobs and places are archived, not erased - look in the Archived section and swipe to Restore. A job that has been used can never be deleted, only archived, so its cost history stays.

Where's the camera? It won't scan

iPhone Settings → LocaTrack → Camera must be on. Good light helps, and hold steady about a hand-width from the label.
